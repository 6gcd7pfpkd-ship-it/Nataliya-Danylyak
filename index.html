About me

From cobblestones of Lviv to the skyline of Chicago

I grew up in Lviv — a city of coffee, old streets and warm traditions. Moving to Chicago with my family opened a whole new chapter, and I started sharing it: the first impressions, the funny differences, the everyday discoveries.

Today my blog is a guide and a diary in one. I show how life in the USA really looks, find places worth your weekend, take you along on family trips, visit events and share beauty finds that actually work.

My audience trusts me because I only recommend what I've tried myself. That's exactly what I offer brands: a genuine voice and content people want to watch.
